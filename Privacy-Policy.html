Privacy Overview

Your Information, Explained Clearly

This Privacy Policy applies to ffbiocodes.com, including our pages, tools, and contact options. It explains the limited information that may be handled when you use the site and does not apply to third-party websites with their own privacy notices.

01 · Website Owner

Website Owner

FF Bio Code operates ffbiocodes.com. For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, visitors can contact us using the contact details provided at the end of this policy.

02 · Information

Information We Collect

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably needed to operate the site and respond to visitors. Depending on how you use the website, this may include:

  • Basic contact information, such as your name and email address, when you voluntarily submit it
  • The content of messages, feedback, or requests that you choose to send
  • Ratings and optional review feedback that visitors voluntarily submit through the website's review form
  • Limited technical and usage information—such as an IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, pages visited, and basic usage information—generated by browsers, hosting, security, or enabled third-party services
  • Cookie or similar-technology information when those technologies are used

03 · Purpose

How We Use Your Information

Information is used only for legitimate website-related purposes, such as to:

  • Respond to questions, messages, and support requests
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website and its content
  • Understand general usage patterns when analytics or advertising services are enabled
  • Detect and reduce spam, abuse, fraud, or technical problems
  • Meet applicable legal, security, or record-keeping obligations
04 · Technologies

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. FF Bio Code and services used on the site may use cookies or similar technologies for essential site functions, security, measurement, and advertising where applicable.

Where required by applicable law, we may provide users with controls to manage or consent to non-essential cookies and similar technologies.

You can manage, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how some services, preferences, or advertisements work.

05 · Google Services

Google AdSense & Analytics

If Google AdSense advertisements are displayed on FF Bio Code, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies as part of ad delivery. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. If analytics services, including Google Analytics, are enabled, they may provide aggregated information about visits and general site use. We use aggregated reporting to understand and improve the website; we do not use analytics reports to identify individual visitors. Google and other enabled advertising or analytics providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ads Settings. You can also manage cookies in your browser settings. Advertising and analytics providers process information under their own privacy policies.
06 · Third Parties

Third-Party Services and Links

FF Bio Code may rely on third-party services to operate or support parts of the website, including hosting and infrastructure providers, security providers, FormSubmit for contact-form or email processing, and advertising or analytics/measurement providers where enabled.

Our website may also contain links to third-party websites. We do not control their privacy practices, content, availability, or terms. Please review the privacy policy of every external website you visit.

07 · Security

Data Security

We use reasonable measures to help protect information handled through the website. However, no internet transmission, email message, or electronic storage method is completely secure. Please avoid sending passwords, account-recovery details, payment information, or other sensitive information through our contact options.

08 · Retention

How Long We Keep Information

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide website-related services, maintain security, comply with applicable legal obligations, or resolve disputes. When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely disposed of.

09 · Children

Children's Privacy

FF Bio Code is not intentionally directed to children, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the site, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

10 · Your Choices

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have choices or rights regarding personal information you voluntarily shared with us. You may:

  • Ask us to correct or delete contact information you previously submitted, where we can reasonably identify it
  • Withdraw consent for future contact by following up with us
  • Manage advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings or other vendor controls, where available
  • Manage or delete cookies through your browser settings
  • Contact us with questions about how information is handled

To make a privacy-related request, contact us at support@ffbiocodes.com and provide sufficient information for us to understand and process your request.
