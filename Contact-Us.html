Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

How long does it take to get a reply?

We aim to reply to genuine enquiries within 24–48 hours. Response times can be longer during busy periods.

Do you help with game issues?

We can help with FF Bio Code website tools and content. For game accounts, payments, or gameplay issues, please contact Garena's official support.

What topics can I contact you about?

You can contact us about website feedback, non-working content, feature ideas, accessibility concerns, or partnership enquiries.

Is FF Bio Code an official Free Fire website?

No. FF Bio Code is an independent fan-made resource and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Garena or Free Fire.

Can I suggest a new feature?

Yes. We welcome practical suggestions that can make our bio codes, tools, or guides clearer and more useful.

Where can I follow updates?

New tools and content updates are published on FF Bio Codes. You can also use this form to ask about a specific update.
